The original GBIF-format download was tab-separated. The extract selects `speciesKey = 2475532`. No deduplication, geographic cleaning, quality filtering, or independent validation of observations was applied.

These are occurrence records, not counts of individual birds or population estimates. Yearly differences may reflect observation effort or changes in contributing datasets.

Original fields are preserved as text; empty fields are null. Coordinates and year fields can be converted to numbers. `eventDate` can contain date intervals.
